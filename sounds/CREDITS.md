# Sound credits

## keypresses.wav

- Source: Keyboard Soundpack #1, Unicae Games, https://opengameart.org/node/122745
- License: CC0. The pack's readme says "Free to use however you like."
- Recording: Cherry KC 1000, Shure SM7B, post-processed with Izotope Neutron 2

Changes here: 31 of the 32 single keypress clips were used, resampled to 48 kHz stereo,
trimmed before the click, faded out at the tail and evened out in level, then joined into
one file. `keypress-020` was left out for being far quieter than the rest.

## mouse-down.wav, mouse-up.wav

- Source: UI Audio (UI SFX Set), Kenney Vleugels, https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- License: CC0. Files `mouseclick1.ogg` and `mouserelease1.ogg`

Changes here: decoded to 48 kHz stereo and lowered in level to sit under the keys.

## Why this file exists

CC0 asks for no credit. It is here so anyone can check where the sounds came from.
